RED PRAIRIE — ROUND 19b: POST-IMPLEMENTATION BUG REVIEW
====================================================================
You asked me to check Round 19 (two-tier move-out) for bugs and
improvements. I re-read all 5 changed files plus the pricing engine
line-by-line, re-ran the price harness with new cases (sqft + add-on
combos, both tiers blocked, both guarantee types), and grepped the
whole repo for any leftover single-tier or hourly references. This
file covers what I found and what I fixed. 2 files changed as a result;
both already re-verified with node --check and the price harness.


FIXED (2 real bugs)
--------------------------------------------------------
1. index.html — stale Google-facing pricing.
   The homepage's schema.org structured data (the JSON-LD block search
   engines read, separate from anything a visitor sees) still had ONE
   "Factory Reset™ Move-Out Clean (Starting At) — $199" offer. That
   number predates even the old single-tier model's real prices
   ($299–$599), and after Round 19 it didn't correspond to either real
   tier at all. Left alone, this is what a search result or a rich
   snippet could show someone before they ever reach your site — a
   number nobody would actually be quoted.
   FIXED: replaced it with two accurate offers —
     "Move-Out Cleaning: Inspection Ready (Starting At)" — $299
     "Move-Out Cleaning: Refresh (Starting At)" — $179
   Validated the whole JSON-LD block still parses (python json.loads)
   and every inline <script> on the page still passes node --check.

2. call/index.html — CSR script silently skipped home size for Refresh.
   quoteSayLine() (the line that assembles what the CSR says out loud,
   e.g. "for a 3 bedroom, 2 bathroom home") only mentioned square
   footage for moveout/deep/maintenance — moveoutrefresh was left out
   of that array. Since Refresh has its own sqft surcharge (+$50/+$100/
   +$150), a CSR quoting a large Refresh home would say a bedroom/
   bathroom count but never the size, even on a job where size is
   adding real dollars to the number she just quoted.
   FIXED: added "moveoutrefresh" to that array. Re-checked with
   node --check — clean.
