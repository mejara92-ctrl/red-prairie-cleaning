1. js/rp-pricing-engine.js — THE ACTUAL CHANGE
--------------------------------------------------------
Move-Out is no longer one service with a flat/hourly billing toggle.
It's two services, chosen up front on the service list, same as picking
Deep vs. Basic:

  moveout          "Inspection Ready Move-Out"   (same key as before —
                    no GHL/webhook/analytics migration needed)
  moveoutrefresh    "Move-Out Refresh"             (NEW)

INSPECTION READY — UNCHANGED PRICES. Nothing here was re-priced:
  1-2BR  $299   |  3BR  $399   |  4BR  $499   |  5+BR  $599
  + large-home surcharge: +$100 / +$200 / +$300 (2,200-3,400 sq ft)
  Full checklist (oven, fridge, cabinets, closets, baseboards, interior
  windows, walls, bathrooms, floors). Defend Your Deposit™ guarantee.

MOVE-OUT REFRESH — NEW, roughly 60% of the matching Inspection Ready
tier, rounded to a clean number (a starting estimate, not a measured
rate — see "WORTH DOING NEXT" below):
  1-2BR  $179   |  3BR  $229   |  4BR  $279   |  5+BR  $329
  + large-home surcharge: +$50 / +$100 / +$150 (half of Inspection Ready)
  Lighter scope: kitchen/bath surfaces, appliance exteriors, cabinet/
  closet exteriors, floors, trash out. Does NOT include oven/fridge
  interiors, inside cabinets/closets, baseboards, interior windows, or
  wall spot-cleaning. Satisfaction Guaranteed (quality of what we
  cleaned) instead of Defend Your Deposit — it isn't priced or
  checklisted to survive a landlord inspection, so it doesn't promise to.
  Fridge interior is a $50 add-on for this tier (rpAddonCatalog.fridge —
  already existed, just wasn't offered on Move-Out before).

REMOVED ENTIRELY: Hourly Move-Out ($75/hr, 4hr minimum, RP_MOVEOUT_HOURLY_*).
That was a second way to price the SAME guaranteed job and had drifted
from the flat-rate table's real hours — a customer could get a different
number for the same job depending which path they picked. Simpler to
delete it than keep re-syncing it, especially now that "which product am
I buying" is answered by a real tier choice instead of a mid-flow toggle.
Deleted: rpIsMoveoutHourly(), RP_MOVEOUT_HOURLY_RATE/MIN_HOURS/
MAX_HOURS/CLEANERS, the moveoutMode state field, the "moveouthours" step.

BOTH TIERS SHARE: the same bedrooms/bathrooms/sqft questions, the same
"moveoutintent" (rental vs. selling) question, the same water/A-C/mold-
pests hard-stop questionnaire (rpMoveoutBlocked() now checks both
services — if the job can't happen as booked, that's true regardless of
which tier someone picked), the same 2-cleaner crew, the same military
discount, and the same $150 one-visit floor (both tiers price above it
at every size, so it never actually bites).

Guarantee model (rpGuaranteeType()) now has Inspection Ready on
"deposit", Move-Out Refresh on "satisfaction" (the same honest bucket
Deep/Basic already use — quality guaranteed, completeness is not).
