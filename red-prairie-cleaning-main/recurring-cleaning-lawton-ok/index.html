Recurring Cleaning FAQs

Can I start with an affordable maintenance clean?

Yes. The calculator is now designed to keep the first quote realistic. Add-ons are optional, so smaller maintained homes can start with a lower per-visit estimate.

What if my home needs more than maintenance?

If the home has heavy buildup, pet hair, or neglected areas, we may recommend adding a deep-clean first visit or specific add-ons.

What does "Defend Your Deposit" mean?

It means we clean to the standard most landlords and property managers actually check at move-out: appliances inside and out, bathrooms fully sanitized, baseboards, floors, and more. And if a landlord ever flags something we missed, we're not going to argue about it — text or call us within 48 hours and we'll come back and make it right, free.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Red Prairie Cleaning is licensed and insured, so you're covered in the rare event anything is damaged during a cleaning.

Questions? Need Help Choosing A Schedule?

Text us your home size, pets, and how often you want service. We can help you choose weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly.
