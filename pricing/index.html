What's actually on the line. Around Lawton a security deposit runs about a month's rent — roughly $600 on a one-bedroom, $850–$900 on a two, $1,100–$1,200 on a three, and $1,300–$1,400 on a four. At every one of those sizes the clean costs less than half of what you're trying to get back.

Deposits vary by landlord and lease — these are typical local figures, not a quote on yours. And to be plain about it: we re-clean what we missed. We don't decide whether a landlord returns a deposit, and we'd never promise that.

Worked example. A 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom home in normal condition: $299. Two cleaners for six hours, the full interior, bathrooms included at no extra charge. Nothing on the checklist is billed separately at the end. If a home turns out to be far heavier than described, the crew prices that with you on arrival before starting — you can approve it or reschedule.

A few quick facts before your price

We ask three simple yes/no questions before your price: is the water on, is the A/C working, and any signs of mold or pests. It's not about how dirty the home is; it's just confirming the job can happen as booked. If any answer says otherwise, we'll call you directly instead of guessing at a number online.
