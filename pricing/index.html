A few quick facts before your price

We ask three simple yes/no questions before your price: is the water on, is the A/C working, and any signs of mold or pests. It's not about how dirty the home is; it's just confirming the job can happen as booked. If any answer says otherwise, we'll call you directly instead of guessing at a number online. This applies to both Move-Out tiers.

Which one do I need?

If a landlord, property manager, or housing office is going to inspect the home after you leave, book Inspection Ready — it's the only tier backed by Defend Your Deposit. If you're just handing the place over with nobody checking it against a list, Move-Out Refresh gets it clean for less. You can switch tiers right up until the crew is booked.
