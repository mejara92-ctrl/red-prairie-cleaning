RED PRAIRIE CLEANING — V22 CHANGELOG
/book only — no other pages touched.

1. Native back button / gesture now steps back through the funnel
   -------------------------------------------------------------
   Previously the on-screen Back button mutated state directly and the
   device's native back button/gesture had no hook into the funnel at
   all — using it would leave /book entirely and drop all progress.

   Now every forward step (service select, each flow step, entering the
   calendar, moving from date-select to time-select) pushes a browser
   history entry. The on-screen Back button and the "Choose a different
   date" link now both just call history.back(); a single popstate
   listener restores the prior screen's state and re-renders. This means
   the native back button/gesture and the on-screen Back button now do
   the exact same thing — reliably.

   Backing out of the very first funnel step now correctly leaves /book
   for whatever page the person arrived from, matching normal browser
   behavior.

2. Session resume via sessionStorage
   ----------------------------------
   A refresh, accidental tab close, low battery, or an incoming call no
   longer resets the whole funnel. The person's own answers (service,
   bedrooms/bathrooms, condition/frequency, every add-on choice, and
   every contact-detail field they've typed) are saved to sessionStorage
   after every screen change and restored on reload.

   Deliberately NOT persisted: loading flags, fetched slot availability,
   in-flight errors, or a previously selected calendar date/time — if
   someone resumes into the calendar step, it always re-fetches live
   availability and starts at date-selection rather than trusting a
   stale time slot that may no longer be open.

   Sessions expire after 3 hours — an old tab reopened the next day
   starts fresh rather than surprising someone with yesterday's partial
   quote.

   An explicit ?service= URL parameter (ad click, landing-page CTA)
   always wins over a resumable session — someone deliberately starting
   a specific flow doesn't get pulled back into an old one.

   The saved session is cleared the moment a booking is actually
   confirmed, so a completed booking is never resumable and doesn't
   linger in storage.
