Say this first

Buy yourself a few seconds

“That's a good question — let me make sure I give you the right answer rather than guess.”

Then look it up. Nobody minds a short pause on the phone. Guessing is the only wrong move.

If you still don't know

Take a number and call them back

“Let me check that properly and call you right back — what's the best number for you?”

This is always allowed and it is never the wrong answer. Put what they asked into the notes when you end the call, so whoever rings back knows what it's about.

Never do these three

The ones that cost money

Never guess a price. Whatever you say out loud is what they will expect to pay, and we have to honour it.

Never promise they'll get their deposit back. We come back and re-clean if something was missed. Whether a landlord returns a deposit is not ours to promise.

Never push past a red box. If the screen has flagged the job, take a name and number and let the office sort it out.

Still stuck

Hand it over, warmly

Take the name, the number, and what they asked. End the call kindly and pass it on. A lead called back within the hour is a good outcome. A confident wrong answer is not.

Reminder

You cannot break this

Back works at any point, every answer can be changed, and the price recalculates itself. Nothing reaches the office until you book the job or log the call at the end.
