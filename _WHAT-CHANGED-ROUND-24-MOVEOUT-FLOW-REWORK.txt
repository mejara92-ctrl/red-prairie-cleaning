1. THE NEW /book MOVE-OUT FLOW
--------------------------------------------------------
Old (round 23):  bedrooms+bathrooms (merged) -> sqft -> rental/selling
                 -> quick facts -> contact gate -> tier comparison ->
                 add-ons -> estimate -> lead -> calendar   (10 steps)

New (round 24):  bedrooms -> bathrooms -> contact gate -> quick facts
                 -> tier comparison -> add-ons -> estimate -> lead ->
                 calendar   (9 steps)

Blocked path:    bedrooms -> bathrooms -> contact gate -> quick facts
                 -> "let's talk it through" callback screen   (4 steps,
                 same count as before -- just reordered)

What moved and why:
  - Square footage is GONE, not hidden. Move-Out is flat bedroom-tier
    pricing now at every size -- no large-home surcharge, no
    sqft-triggered custom quote. You picked this over keeping a
    lightweight big-home toggle. If a genuinely oversized home slips
    through underpriced, that's now something to catch by eye (an
    unusual bed/bath ratio) rather than something the funnel screens
    for -- worth watching for a few weeks.
  - Bedrooms and bathrooms are separate screens again, reversing round
    23's merge. Bathrooms still pre-fills to the included count for
    that bedroom tier as a starting guess, it just does that when you
    land on the bathrooms screen instead of revealing a second question
    in place.
  - "Is this a rental, or are you selling?" is gone from /book entirely.
    /call keeps asking its own copy of this on its own script step
    (unaffected) -- so a phone booking still gets Liz's selling/renting
    nudge and that field in the crew notes, but a self-serve /book lead
    now reports "Not asked" for it, which is accurate. The comparison
    screen's fineprint was tweaked to mention sellers generically
    ("...or you're listing it for sale") so that advice doesn't
    disappear along with the question.
  - Contact gate (name + phone) moved from right before the tier
    comparison to right after bathrooms -- now the very first thing
    after size, before quick facts and before pricing. Practical effect:
    a home that turns out to be blocked (no water, no A/C, mold/pests)
    now hands over a phone number BEFORE finding that out, instead of
    only on the callback screen itself. The callback screen no longer
    re-asks for name/phone when it already has them (which is always,
    now, barring a session-resume edge case) -- it just confirms by
    first name and asks for the callback.


--------------------------------------------------------
2. MOLD/PESTS QUESTION — FIXED THE BACKWARDS POLARITY
--------------------------------------------------------
You flagged it exactly right: "Is the water on?" and "Is the air
conditioning working?" both had Yes = no problem, but "Any signs of
mold or pests?" flipped that -- Yes meant a problem. Someone tapping
through on autopilot could hit Yes/Yes/Yes meaning "everything's fine,
also there's mold," which reads backwards.

FIXED: reworded to "Is the home free of mold or pest issues?" -- Yes now
means no problem on all three questions, so a clean home is Yes/Yes/Yes
straight through and any No is the one that flags it. This is a label
and button-wiring change only; the underlying data your office sees
(water/A/C/mold status on the crew sheet, the webhook, what blocks a
job) is stored exactly the same as before.

3. JUNK HAUL — NOW QUOTED SEPARATELY, LIKE HANDYMAN REPAIRS
--------------------------------------------------------
Old: half load $100 / full load $200 / oversized (quoted separately).
New: one question -- "Have Trash or Junk to Haul Away?" -- with no
price shown, same "Quoted separately based on volume" treatment as
"Need Small Handyman Repairs?" right below it on the add-ons screen.

Per your note, carpet, garage floor wash, and exterior window pricing
are untouched -- this round only touches junk hauling.

Applied consistently across all three surfaces so nobody gets a
different answer depending on how they book:
  - /book: the half/full/oversized picker is replaced by the one
    checkbox described above.
  - /call: Liz's picker collapsed from three priced options to one
    ("Junk/trash to haul away (quoted separately, NOT in the total)"),
    so she never quotes a flat $100/$200 that a self-serve customer
    couldn't have gotten online.
  - /pricing: the "$100 / $200" row is gone; the add-ons table just
    says "Junk hauling — Quoted separately," and the fine print at the
    bottom of that section was reworded since junk hauling is now the
    thing on that page that can't be priced online (used to be just the
    oversized case).

Technically this rides on infrastructure that was already there: round
21 built a single "quoted item, $0 in the math, flagged everywhere" path
for the oversized-load case specifically. This round just makes that the
ONLY path for junk, instead of one of three. That means the summary
text, the webhook field, the crew sheet, and /call's job details all
already knew how to show a quoted-but-unpriced line item -- nothing new
had to be built for those, which is also why this change touched
noticeably less code than the flow rework above.
