RED PRAIRIE CLEANING — V23 CHANGELOG
/book only — design pass from the visual audit.

1. Fixed a real contrast failure
   -----------------------------
   --faint (#9d9287) measured at 2.71:1 against the paper background —
   below WCAG AA's 4.5:1 minimum for body text, and it was set on some of
   the smallest text on the page (9.5–10.5px). Swapped every text usage
   to --muted (#71685f, 4.86:1 — passes AA). Confirmed live in-browser on
   the add-on detail sheets ("· $50 per room" style notes), which render
   at the corrected color now.

   Honest note: while auditing this, I found the two ".rp-footer-note"
   disclaimer lines ("Confirmation sends only after your appointment
   time is booked" / "You'll receive confirmation by text and email")
   are actually set to display:none on both screens they're written for
   (step-lead and step-calendar) — they were never visible to a real
   user, contrast issue or not. Left them in place since removing markup
   wasn't asked for, but flagging it: it's dead copy sitting in the
   template, not a live bug.

2. Trimmed the motion
   -------------------
   Removed the staggered per-row "rise in" animation that replayed on
   every one of 8 steps (each element on screen fading/rising in with an
   incremental delay, every single transition). Kept the one directional
   slide animation (forward steps slide from the right, back steps from
   the left) since that's functional — it reinforces which way you're
   moving through the funnel. The decorative layer on top of it is gone.
   Also removed the now-unused rpRise keyframe.

3. Reduced background atmosphere
   ------------------------------
   Each screen was painting its own two radial gradients on top of the
   two already coming from the app shell underneath — four overlapping
   glows per screen, repeated at every step. Screens now use a plain
   paper gradient and let the single ambient glow already on .rp-app
   show through. Same warmth, a quarter of the layering, and one less
   thing competing with the actual content for attention.

4. Added a real signature element
   -------------------------------
   The price-reveal card had a giant, nearly-invisible "RP" monogram
   watermark (opacity .045, and actually already suppressed sitewide by
   a later CSS override from a prior pass — display:none !important —
   so it hadn't been rendering at all). Replaced it with an inline SVG
   wheat-sheaf mark — three fanned blades with a small grain cluster —
   pulled from the same visual language as your actual logo mark, sized
   large and bled off the corner of the card at 8% opacity. It's the one
   moment on the page that's unmistakably Red Prairie rather than "a
   nice premium checkout," on the one screen (the price reveal) that
   deserves it most.
