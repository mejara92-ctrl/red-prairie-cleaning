Two Options. Both Priced By The Hour.

We charge $40 an hour for a detailer, which is the same rate we charge for a cleaner in your house. That is the whole pricing model, and it is why there are two options instead of ten: you are choosing how much time the car gets, not which package has the most words in it.

Standard is three hours — $120. A full interior vacuum including seats and carpets, the dash, console, doors and trim wiped down, interior glass, and door jambs. This is the right choice for a car that is basically fine and needs resetting.

Deep clean is six hours — $240. Everything above, plus shampooed seats and carpets and the vents and crevices a wipe-down cannot reach. If there are stains, or a smell you have stopped noticing, this is the one that actually fixes it rather than covering it.

Pet hair is $50 on either one. We price it separately because it is the one thing that reliably blows up the time — hair worked into upholstery and carpet is slow, manual work that no vacuum lifts. Charging it separately means people without a dog are not quietly paying for one.

What Interior Detailing Actually Covers

Every line below is in both options unless it says deep clean. Nothing here is billed separately once you have booked.

Both options

  • Full interior vacuum — seats, carpets, mats, boot
  • Dash, console, cupholders and controls wiped down
  • Door panels, handles and trim
  • Interior glass, including the inside of the windscreen
  • Door jambs
  • Rubbish out, mats cleaned and put back

Deep clean adds

  • Shampooed seats and carpets
  • Vents, seams and crevices
  • Seat rails and under-seat areas
  • Headliner spot-cleaning where it is safe to do so
  • Longer on stains, which is what stains actually need

What we do not do: exterior washing, waxing, paint correction, engine bays or headlight restoration. We are a cleaning company, and the inside of a car responds to the same work we do in a house. The outside is somebody else's trade and we would rather say so than do it badly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you wash the outside of the car?

No. This is interior only — no exterior wash, wax or paint correction. We are a cleaning company, and the inside of a car is the part that responds to the same work we do in a house.

How much does interior car detailing cost in Lawton?

$120 for three hours or $240 for six, with one detailer. That is $40 an hour, the same rate as our house cleaning. Pet hair removal is $50 on top of whichever one you pick, and it is the only thing that changes the price.

What is the difference between the three-hour and six-hour detail?

The three-hour standard is a full vacuum, wipe-down of the dash, console, doors and trim, interior glass and door jambs. The six-hour deep clean adds shampooed seats and carpets, plus the vents and crevices a wipe-down cannot reach. If there are stains or a smell, you want the six.

Why is pet hair priced separately?

Because it is the one thing that reliably blows up the time. Hair embedded in upholstery and carpet is slow, manual work that a vacuum will not lift. Pricing it separately means people without pets are not quietly paying for it.

Where do you do the work?

In your driveway. We bring what we need. If we are already at the house for a cleaning, the car gets done in the same visit.

Is it cheaper to add it to a house cleaning?

It is the same price either way, but adding it to a cleaning means one trip instead of two — better for scheduling, and you are not paying for a separate visit.

Do I need to be there?

No, as long as we can get to the car and it is unlocked or you have left a key. Most people leave it on the drive and get on with their day.
