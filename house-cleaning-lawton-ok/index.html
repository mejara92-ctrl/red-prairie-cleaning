Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in house cleaning?

House cleaning commonly includes bathrooms, kitchen surfaces, dusting, vacuuming, mopping, exterior appliance wipe-downs, and high-touch areas. Deep cleaning adds more detail such as baseboards, inside oven, inside microwave, and buildup areas.

Do you offer deep cleaning in Lawton?

Yes. Red Prairie Cleaning offers deep cleaning in Lawton for first-time cleans, seasonal resets, and homes with heavier buildup.

Can I book recurring house cleaning?

Yes. Recurring cleaning can be discussed after the first cleaning or by texting Red Prairie Cleaning with your preferred schedule and home details.

How much does house cleaning cost in Lawton?

Pricing depends on cleaning type, home size, number of bedrooms and bathrooms, condition, add-ons, access, travel, and pet hair. The online estimator gives a starting price before booking.

What does "Defend Your Deposit" mean?

It means we clean to the standard most landlords and property managers actually check at move-out: appliances inside and out, bathrooms fully sanitized, baseboards, floors, and more. And if a landlord ever flags something we missed, we're not going to argue about it — text or call us within 48 hours and we'll come back and make it right, free.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Red Prairie Cleaning is licensed and insured, so you're covered in the rare event anything is damaged during a cleaning.
