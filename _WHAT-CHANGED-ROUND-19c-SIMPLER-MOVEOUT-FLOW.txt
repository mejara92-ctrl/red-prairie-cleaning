--------------------------------------------------------
1. THE NEW /book FLOW
--------------------------------------------------------
Old: service list showed Inspection Ready and Move-Out Refresh as two
separate rows. Picking either one asked a "what's included" screen,
then rental/selling, THEN bedrooms/bathrooms/sqft/add-ons/questions,
THEN the price for whichever tier you'd already committed to.

New: one "Move-Out Cleaning" row on the service list. Picking it goes
straight into:
  Bedrooms -> Bathrooms -> Square footage (no price shown) ->
  Rental/selling question -> Water/A-C/mold quick facts ->
  [Move-Out Refresh vs. Inspection Ready, side by side, real prices] ->
  Add-ons (tier-specific) -> Final price -> Book

The tier decision moved from the very first tap (before the customer
knows anything about either option) to right before the price reveal
(after they've described their actual home, when a real $229 vs. $399
means something instead of two "From $X" teasers).

Internally: rpState.service holds "moveout" as a neutral placeholder
while collecting bedrooms/bathrooms/sqft (that data doesn't depend on
which tier gets picked), then becomes the real, final tier the moment
the customer taps a card on the new comparison screen
(rpChooseMoveoutTier() in /book, rpMoveoutTierBasePrice() in the
engine computes both real numbers for that screen without disturbing
the "official" service value until they pick). A new
moveoutTierChosen flag distinguishes "just started, tier undecided"
from "actually picked Inspection Ready" — both look identical as a
raw string, so the flag is what keeps the comparison screen from
looking like Inspection Ready was already pre-selected on first
arrival.

The Contact Gate (name + phone, before any price shows) now fires
right before the NEW comparison screen instead of before Add-ons —
that's the earliest point either real price appears now, and the gate
exists specifically to close that leak. The partial-lead note sent to
the office at that moment now shows both tier prices ("Inspection
Ready $399.00 / Refresh $229.00 (tier not yet chosen)") instead of
one number that would have implied a tier nobody picked yet.

/call (the CSR phone console) is UNCHANGED in structure — it still
lists Inspection Ready and Move-Out Refresh as two separate rows, on
purpose. A CSR can just ask "will a landlord inspect this?" in one
breath and pick the right one directly; the multi-step, side-by-side
reveal is a self-serve-website problem, not a phone-call one. Say the
word if you want /call restructured to match.


--------------------------------------------------------
2. THE NEW COMPARISON SCREEN
--------------------------------------------------------
Two cards, side by side (stacks to one column only on the narrowest
phones so nothing gets crushed): tier name, real starting price for
the bedrooms/bathrooms/size just entered, guarantee type (Defend Your
Deposit™ vs. Satisfaction Guaranteed), 4 short "what's included"
bullets pulled from the same content used elsewhere on the site, and —
on the Refresh card specifically — one line naming what's NOT
included (oven/fridge interiors, inside cabinets, baseboards, interior
windows), so nobody picks it thinking it's the full checklist. A "Not
sure?" line underneath repeats the one-question test (is a landlord/PM
inspecting it) that already exists on /call and /pricing.

If the home is over 3,400 sq ft or the water/A-C/mold questions
blocked the job, both cards show "Custom" instead of a number — same
custom-quote handling as everywhere else on the site, not a dead end.
