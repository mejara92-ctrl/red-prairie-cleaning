Is the instant estimate the final price?

It is based on the size and options you select. We contact you before changing it for severe buildup, heavy trash, unusual access, biohazards, or work outside the selected scope.

What does a Move-Out Cleaning include?

Everything inside the home. Inside the oven and the refrigerator, inside cabinets and closets, bathrooms scrubbed top to bottom, baseboards, doors and trim, interior windows, sills and tracks, ceiling fans, vents and light fixtures, and all floors throughout. There is one Move-Out Cleaning and one price for your home's size — no lighter tier to compare against and nothing on that list billed separately. The four things it doesn't cover are all outside the home: exterior windows, the garage floor, carpet extraction and junk removal, and you can add any of them while you book. Every move-out is backed by our Defend Your Deposit™ guarantee.

How do booking and arrival work?

No deposit is required. Same-day openings are often available, and we use a 30-minute arrival window so the team can finish the prior appointment properly and travel safely.

Do I need to be home or have the property empty?

You do not need to be home. Move-in and move-out cleans work best in an empty property; provide a key, lockbox, property-manager contact, or other access instructions.

How should I prepare?

Remove personal items, important documents, and loose trash unless junk hauling was selected. Water and electricity must be available.

What do the optional add-ons cover?

Tap any add-on for its exact scope and price. Garage floor washing requires an empty garage; exterior window washing has a basic and a premium (screens included) option; and junk or trash hauling is quoted separately based on volume rather than a flat price, since a truckload can vary so much.

How long will the cleaning take?

Your estimate shows the expected time and team size. Actual time varies with the home, access, and condition.

What if something is missed?

If we miss something in the selected scope, contact us within 48 hours and we come back and fix it, free — that's Defend Your Deposit™ on every Move-Out Cleaning, and our Satisfaction Guarantee on everything else.
