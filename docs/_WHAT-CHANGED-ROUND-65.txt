===============================================================================
"Has the place seen better days? Instead put How's the condition of the home,
standard or heavy build up. Apply to website as well."

                     [ Normal / Just needs a good clean ]

    WAS (/call):   Has the place seen better days?   [ Yes ] [ No ]

    NOW (both):    How's the condition of the home?

WHY IT IS BETTER THAN WHAT I WROTE IN ROUND 58
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Standard" is the word this site already uses for exactly this idea, in every
other place it appears: "standard home in standard condition" on the estimate,
the promise strip, the landing pages and the fine print. So the word the
customer taps here is now the same word they read everywhere else. My idiom
was friendlier and made the customer translate.

It also fixed a shape mismatch. /call was still a Yes/No, so the console and
the website asked the same question two different ways -- and "Has the place
seen better days? [Yes] [No]" is a sentence a CSR has to decode before she can
tap. She now taps the word the caller just said.
