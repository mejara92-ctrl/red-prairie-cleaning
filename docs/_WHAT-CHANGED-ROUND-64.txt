ROUND 64 — FOUR THINGS CUT FROM THE PHONE
===============================================================================
"the mobile version needs to be simpler if possible"

Asked which four to cut rather than guessing a fifth time. All four approved.
Every one is PHONE-ONLY. The desktop board keeps all of it — that is the
whole point of having two shells.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE TWO ADMIN FIELDS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Why are they moving? (Renting / Selling)" and "Property manager or realtor
(optional)". They were the only two things on the home screen that change
nothing: not the price, not whether we can book it, not what the crew does on
arrival. The code's own comment said "context for the crew, doesn't change the
price".

On a 1,400px desktop column they cost nothing. On a phone they were two of
seven questions, and one was a text field to type into while a stranger talks.

Home screen: 7 questions -> 5. Step body 929px -> 730px.

The fields still exist on rpState, still reset, still reach the office in the
webhook. They are simply not asked for on a phone. Anyone working the
property-manager referral channel works it from a desk.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. "TEXT THEM THIS PRICE / SAVE LEAD & END" — ONLY WHERE IT APPLIES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Was on all seven steps. "Text them this price" means nothing before there is a
price, and "Save lead & end" is a thing she does at the end — so on five of
seven screens it was a pair of buttons that either could not fire or was not
what she was doing.

Now: quote, address, calendar, and any flagged call. Gone from the other three.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. ONE DOOR TO THE STEP LIST, NOT TWO
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The progress bar said "All steps" and the dock has a "Steps" button. Two doors
to the same sheet, eight inches apart, on a tool whose main complaint has been
falling into windows.

The bar is a label now — where she is, and nothing else. It also lost the
pointer and the 48px tap height that advertised it as a button. The dock's
Steps button is the single way in.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. NO "MORE DETAIL" ON THE SCRIPT CARD
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
It sat inside the one block she reads out loud, so a mis-tap mid-sentence
dropped a paragraph of coaching on top of the words she was saying. The same
coaching is searchable under Help, which is a deliberate trip rather than an
accident. Desktop keeps it.
