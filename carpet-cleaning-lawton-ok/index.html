FAQ

Carpet cleaning questions.

A few quick answers before you book.

How long does carpet take to dry?

Most carpets dry within about 6–12 hours depending on humidity, airflow, carpet thickness, and how soiled the carpet was before cleaning.

Can you remove every stain?

No company can honestly guarantee every stain will fully disappear. Results depend on the stain type, age, carpet fiber, previous cleaning attempts, and carpet condition. Many stains can be improved dramatically.

Do you clean pet odors?

Yes, we offer pet odor treatment as an add-on. Severe urine contamination may require extra treatment and sometimes cannot be fully corrected with surface cleaning alone.

How often should carpets be professionally cleaned?

Most homes benefit from professional carpet cleaning every 6–12 months. Homes with kids, pets, allergies, or heavy traffic may need it more often.

Do you also clean the house?

Yes. Red Prairie Cleaning also offers house cleaning, deep cleaning, move-out cleaning, and recurring cleaning services.
