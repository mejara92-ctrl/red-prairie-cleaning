Frequently Asked Questions

How much does carpet cleaning start at?

Carpet cleaning is $50 per room on the online estimator, with a two-room minimum. Final pricing can vary based on room size, carpet condition, pet treatment, stairs, area rugs, and travel.

Do you offer pet odor treatment?

Yes. Pet stain treatment and deodorizer are available as carpet cleaning add-ons during the estimate process.

What equipment do you use?

Red Prairie Cleaning uses professional Mytee hot water extraction equipment with pre-spray and wand cleaning.

Can I book another service from this carpet cleaning page?

Yes. The online estimator lets customers choose move-out cleaning, move-in cleaning, deep cleaning, maintenance cleaning, carpet cleaning from any Red Prairie Cleaning service page.

What does "Defend Your Deposit" mean?

It means we clean to the standard most landlords and property managers actually check at move-out: appliances inside and out, bathrooms fully sanitized, baseboards, floors, and more. And if a landlord ever flags something we missed, we're not going to argue about it — text or call us within 48 hours and we'll come back and make it right, free.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Red Prairie Cleaning is licensed and insured, so you're covered in the rare event anything is damaged during a cleaning.
