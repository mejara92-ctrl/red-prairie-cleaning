Quick Questions

What does "Defend Your Deposit" mean?

It means we clean to the standard most landlords and property managers actually check at move-out: appliances inside and out, bathrooms fully sanitized, baseboards, floors, and more. And if a landlord ever flags something we missed, we're not going to argue about it — text or call us within 48 hours and we'll come back and make it right, free.

Do I really need a professional move-out clean?

Some leases require it, some don't — but either way, a documented, top-to-bottom clean is the easiest way to protect your deposit and avoid a dispute with your landlord after you've already moved. Most of our move-out customers are military PCS families, renters, and sellers who want one less thing that can go wrong on moving day.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Red Prairie Cleaning is licensed and insured, so you're covered in the rare event anything is damaged during a cleaning.

Do I need to be home?

No. Many move-out customers are already gone. You can provide access instructions, lockbox details, or coordinate with your property manager.

Is the home supposed to be empty?

For best results, yes. Empty homes clean faster and more thoroughly. We can still work around items, but heavy clutter or trash may change the scope.

Do you clean carpets?

Yes. We use professional hot-water extraction, not a rental steam cleaner. It is $75 per carpeted room and can be added during the estimate.

Do you remove window screens?

No. Exterior window basic wash is $100 and does not include screen removal.

Can I text instead of booking online?

Yes. Text us at (580) 215-0915. Texting is a great option if you have photos, special access instructions, or a property manager contact.

Can you handle last-minute cleanings?

Yes. We can often accommodate same-day and next-day appointments for move-outs, deep cleanings, carpet cleaning, and property manager turnovers. If the online calendar does not show the time you need, call or text us and we will do our best to get you scheduled.

Questions? Text Us Directly.

Send your address, bedroom count, and any photos. We can help confirm the right service before you book.
