========================================================

WHAT WAS WRONG IN THE ROUND 2 ZIP
--------------------------------------------------------
rpSubmitContactGate() called rpSendEstimateToGoHighLevel(), which is a
NO-OP STUB — it sets two flags and returns without sending anything:

    function rpSendEstimateToGoHighLevel(extra = {}) {
      /* Calendar-only confirmation gate: ... */
      rpState.estimateSent = true;
      rpState.preBookingWebhookSent = true;
      return true;
    }

So the gate captured a name and phone into rpState and they never left
the browser. The gate was decorative. Fixed below.

This also answers "where do unfinished bookings go?" — before this
round, NOWHERE. /book is the only endpoint the site called, and it
hard-requires a valid appointment startTime (400s without one). Anyone
who didn't reach the calendar and pick a slot vanished with no contact,
no note, no tag.

ROUND 9 — MINIMUM CHARGE
========================================================
BRIEF: "$150 minimum per thing we clean." Analysis found 22 price
points below $150 — ALL Basic Cleaning. Worst case $99.90 (weekly,
<=1000 sq ft, with military discount).

DECISIONS MADE (each one is reversible — flagged for veto)

1. RAISED THE PUBLISHED PRICE RATHER THAN HIDING A FLOOR
   Basic tier 1 base: $139 -> $150.
   A silent floor would contradict the whole position — the pricing
   page said $139 while the estimator charged $150. "The price you see
   is the price you pay" has to survive contact with the price list.
   The floor stays only as a safety net for edge cases.
   NOTE: this leaves a $5 step from t1 ($150) to t2 ($155) where other
   steps are ~$20. Cosmetically odd, same as the deep $255/$259 gap you
   chose to keep. Left alone rather than repricing the whole ladder.

2. TWO FLOORS, NOT ONE
   RP_ONE_TIME_MIN            = 150
   RP_RECURRING_MIN_PER_VISIT = 115

   Deliberately different. A one-time visit IS the whole relationship
   so it must stand alone. On a recurring plan the ACCOUNT is the unit
   of economics: a weekly customer at $120/visit is ~$6,200/year and is
   one of the best accounts on the book. A blanket $150 would have
   refused that business for no reason.

3. FLOOR APPLIES AFTER THE MILITARY DISCOUNT
   Applied before it, the discount punches straight through the floor
   ($150 becomes $135). CONSEQUENCE, ACCEPTED: a customer already at
   the floor gets no military discount. Basic t1 one-time is $150 with
   or without it. Unavoidable with a hard floor — the alternative is
   letting the floor be broken.

4. FLOOR COVERS THE CLEANING ONLY, NOT ADD-ONS
   Add-ons stack on top, so a thin base can't ride in on an expensive
   junk haul that carries its own disposal cost.

NEW IN THE ENGINE
   RP_ONE_TIME_MIN, RP_RECURRING_MIN_PER_VISIT
   rpIsRecurringBooking(), rpServiceFloorCents(),
   rpNetServiceCents(), rpFloorApplied()
   rpFinalPriceCents / rpMaintenanceBasePerVisit / rpMonthlyTotal /
   rpFirstVisitTotalCents all route through rpNetServiceCents().

PRICING PAGE UPDATED TO MATCH
   Basic tier 1 now published at $150; minimum stated in the hero
   promises and the footer fine print; Basic table gained the condition
   ladder and the first-visit Deep note (round 8 changes that hadn't
   reached the page yet).

   Headline prices UNCHANGED: move-out 1500 3/2 $379 / $354 military,

NOT CHANGED, AND WHY
